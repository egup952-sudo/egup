How faith becomes action

Programs

Eight ministry initiatives that carry EGUP's vision into churches, streets and communities throughout the year.

Each EGUP program exists to serve one of our ministry areas, evangelism, prayer, worship, teaching, drama and dance, community outreach and youth ministry. Together they form a full rhythm of fellowship and mission across the year, building up to the Annual Fellowship itself.

01 Annual Fellowship 02 Gospel Outreach 03 Prayer Walks 04 Worship Gatherings 05 Evangelism Missions 06 Community Ministry 07 Bible Teaching 08 Youth Programs

Program 01

Annual Fellowship

The centrepiece of EGUP's calendar, a full day bringing together churches, institutions, youth and believers from across the region for worship, teaching and unity around that year's theme.

Program 02

Gospel Outreach

Organised outreach drives into neighbourhoods, markets and public spaces, carrying the Gospel directly to people where they are.

Program 03

Prayer Walks

Early-morning walks through town and neighbouring areas, praying over homes, schools, businesses and institutions along the way.

Program 04

Worship Gatherings

Dedicated evenings of praise and worship, creating space for God to reveal Himself among His people outside of the main annual event.

Program 05

Evangelism Missions

Focused, short-term missions sending trained teams into specific communities to share the Gospel and follow up with new believers.

Program 06

Community Ministry

Practical service to the wider community, a tangible expression of the Gospel through acts of care, support and presence.

Program 07

Bible Teaching

Structured teaching sessions that ground believers in Scripture, helping them understand and apply God's Word with confidence.

Program 08

Youth Programs

Dedicated gatherings and activities that disciple young people and position the next generation for lifelong ministry.
