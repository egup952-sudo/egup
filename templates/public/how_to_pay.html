How to pay

EGUP membership can be paid two ways. Both are checked before your membership is confirmed, nothing is marked paid without EGUP verifying it first.

{% if automatic_available %}

M-Pesa Express (automatic)

When you register or renew, choose "Pay with M-Pesa" and a payment prompt is sent straight to your phone. Enter your M-Pesa PIN and your payment is confirmed automatically.

{% endif %} {% if manual_available %}

Pay via M-Pesa PayBill (manual)

Go to M-Pesa on your phone, choose Lipa na M-Pesa → Pay Bill, and enter:

PayBill: {{ settings.manual_payment_paybill }}

{% if settings.manual_payment_till %}

Till: {{ settings.manual_payment_till }}

{% endif %}

Account: {{ settings.manual_payment_account_note }}

After paying, come back to your registration or renewal page and enter your M-Pesa transaction code. An EGUP administrator checks it against our records, you'll see "awaiting verification" until it's confirmed.

{% endif %}
