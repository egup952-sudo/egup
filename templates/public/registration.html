Common questions

Registration FAQ

How much does registration cost, and what does it cover?

Registration is a one-time fee shown on this page, valid for one year of membership. It covers your place in the EGUP fellowship and access to member updates and events throughout the year.

What's the difference between the two payment options?

Paying with M-Pesa (automatic) sends a payment prompt straight to your phone and confirms instantly once you enter your PIN. Paying manually means you pay via our PayBill/Till yourself, then enter your M-Pesa transaction code here, an EGUP admin checks it against our records, which usually only takes a short while.

I paid manually: how long until I'm confirmed?

Manual payments are checked against our M-Pesa records by an admin, not confirmed automatically. You'll see your application number on the confirmation screen, keep it in case you need to follow up.

Do I get a physical membership card?

No, your membership number is your identifier with EGUP. There's no physical card to carry or lose.

What happens when my membership expires?

You can renew any time from the renewal page using your membership number and phone number.

I made a mistake in my details: can I fix it?

Contact us with your application or membership number and we'll help sort it out.
