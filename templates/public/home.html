What is EGUP

A movement of believers, spreading Christ through outreaches.

EGUP is an annual Christian fellowship and outreach program whose purpose is to mobilise believers into active ministry through evangelism, discipleship, prayer, worship, biblical teaching and community outreach.

It brings together churches, institutions, youth and believers to spread the Gospel across Kenya while encouraging spiritual growth, unity and service, spearheaded by Bless A Soul Family Church, which serves as the organisation behind the initiative.

The meaning of EGUP

Four words. One posture before God.

Each letter of EGUP names a movement of the heart, from being planted, to encountering God, to understanding His Word, to living it out.

E
Rooted in Christ

Establishment

Being firmly planted in faith and in fellowship, a stable foundation from which every believer can grow.

Exodus 6:4
G
Encounter

God Revealing Himself

Creating space for God to move among His people through worship, prayer and genuine encounter.

Exodus 6:3
U
Know · Grow · Apply

Understanding His Word

Teaching that builds discernment, helping believers know Scripture and apply it with confidence.

Ephesians 1:18
P
Served · Sent · Purposed

Positioning Ourselves

Taking our place in ministry and mission, ready to serve, to go, and to live out our purpose in Christ.

Jeremiah 1:5 · Esther 4:14 · 1 Corinthians 12:18
