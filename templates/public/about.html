Who we are

A fellowship built to mobilise believers into ministry

EGUP is an annual Christian fellowship and outreach program whose purpose is to mobilise believers into active ministry through evangelism, discipleship, prayer, worship, biblical teaching, and community outreach.

It brings together churches, institutions, youth and believers to spread the Gospel across Kenya while encouraging spiritual growth, unity, and service. EGUP is not a church in itself, it is a program spearheaded by Bless A Soul Family Church, which serves as the organisation carrying the initiative forward each year.

Vision

To restore souls and deliver people from spiritual bondage by raising a generation that walks in truth, purpose, and divine identity through Christ.

Behind the program

Bless A Soul Family Church

EGUP is spearheaded by Bless A Soul Family Church, which organises and coordinates the program each year. While the church carries the responsibility of planning and hosting EGUP, the program itself belongs to the wider body of Christ, open to believers, churches, institutions and youth from across Narok and beyond who share its vision.
